# Code of Conduct

Cybernetic Project Control is an open technical and research project. Participation should remain professional, evidence-oriented and respectful.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas, implementations and evaluation results rather than attacking people.
- State uncertainty and limitations when making technical or empirical claims.
- Welcome reproducible counterexamples, negative results and corrections.
- Respect privacy, confidentiality, licenses and research/engineering safety constraints.
- Do not publish credentials, private datasets, personal records or proprietary project material in issues or pull requests.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, deliberate disclosure of private information, malicious code, deceptive benchmark manipulation, or knowingly fabricated evidence are not acceptable.

## Research and evaluation integrity

Do not modify evaluation oracles solely to make CPC look better. Report failures, timeouts, model/version information and material human interventions. Claims of improved agent behavior should be backed by reproducible evidence.

## Enforcement

Repository maintainers may edit, hide or remove contributions that violate these rules and may restrict participation when necessary to protect contributors or the project. Security-sensitive reports should follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) rather than public issues.
